Common questions

Link building, clearly explained.

Good decisions start with understanding what link building can—and cannot—do.

What is link building?

Link building is the process of earning hyperlinks from other websites to your own. Search engines may use links to discover pages and understand relationships, while visitors can follow them to find useful information.

Why are backlinks important for SEO?

Relevant, editorially earned links can help search engines discover content and provide context about its usefulness or authority. They are one part of SEO—not a substitute for helpful content, sound technical foundations or a good user experience.

How long does link building take?

Timelines vary by industry, content, publisher relationships and competition. Outreach and editorial review can take time, and ranking changes are not guaranteed. A sound campaign is measured over an appropriate period.

Do you guarantee rankings or a specific number of backlinks?

No responsible SEO provider can guarantee search rankings. Link Builders focuses on a transparent process, relevant opportunities and quality-led work rather than promises of guaranteed positions.

Are all backlinks equally valuable?

No. Context, relevance, editorial choice and the quality of the referring page matter. Large volumes of irrelevant or manipulative links can create risk rather than sustainable value.
